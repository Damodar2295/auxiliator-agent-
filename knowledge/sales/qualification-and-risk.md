# Qualification and Deal-Risk Guide

Status: SME-review template.

Assess each opportunity using evidence recorded in Salesforce. Unknown information remains unknown.

## Business case

- What customer problem is being solved?
- What measurable business outcome is expected?
- Why must the customer act now?
- What happens if no decision is made?

## Buying group

- Is there a credible champion with influence and personal motivation?
- Is the economic buyer identified and engaged?
- Are technical, security, legal, procurement, finance, and end-user stakeholders known?
- Is there an authorized signer?

## Decision and purchase process

- What criteria will determine the decision?
- Who participates and who approves?
- Are evaluation, security, legal, procurement, and contracting steps documented?
- Does the target close date align with those steps?

## Solution and competition

- Which requirements are confirmed?
- Which requirements remain unresolved?
- Is the customer comparing a competitor, an internal solution, or doing nothing?
- What differentiation is relevant to the customer's stated outcomes?

## Risk flags

High-priority flags include no customer activity, repeated close-date movement, one-threaded relationships,
missing economic buyer, vague next steps, proposal before discovery, unowned security or legal work, and a
close date unsupported by the purchase process.

The playbook should state evidence, missing evidence, mitigation owner, due date, and confidence. It must not
infer sensitive characteristics or use seller identity as a success predictor.
