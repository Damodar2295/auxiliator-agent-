# Employee Leave Policy

## Overview

Proto provides employees with paid and unpaid leave options to support personal needs, health, family responsibilities, and planned time away from work.

## Leave Types

Employees may request the following types of leave:

### Annual Leave

Annual leave is intended for planned vacations, personal activities, and extended breaks.

Employees should submit annual leave requests in advance whenever possible.

### Sick Leave

Sick leave may be used when an employee is unable to work due to illness or medical requirements.

Employees should notify their manager as early as possible when taking sick leave.

### Personal Leave

Personal leave may be requested for important personal or family matters that require time away from work.

## Leave Request Process

Employees should:

1. Submit the leave request through the approved HR system.
2. Select the appropriate leave type.
3. Provide the requested start and end dates.
4. Provide a brief reason when required.
5. Wait for manager approval before taking planned leave.

## Manager Approval

Managers are responsible for reviewing leave requests based on team requirements, project deadlines, and employee availability.

Approval should be provided fairly and consistently.

## Unplanned Leave

For unexpected absences, employees should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Repeated unexplained absences may be reviewed by HR.

## Holidays

Company holidays are separate from employee leave balances and are communicated through the official company holiday calendar.

## Policy Questions

Employees who have questions about leave balances, eligibility, or exceptions should contact the HR team.
